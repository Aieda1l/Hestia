# Hestia — proposed product brief

Updated October 15, 2026. **PRODUCT DIRECTION APPROVED as part of the written design; not implemented or validated.** Canonical details: [approved Island-first design](superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-island-first-companion-design.md).

**Purpose:** A personally meaningful character who ideally **keeps the user company all day or until they stop it**, with the Dynamic Island as the signature eligible iOS surface. Hestia is inspired by Anima Engine's desktop characters, but native iOS constrains a *single Island Live Activity to eight hours maximum*, and visibility can end sooner. **Companion Mode remains selected until the user turns it off** as locally saved app state; a separate real time-bounded reading/study/creative session may put the character and countdown in Island. Users can adopt/import pet/OC/oshi art, watch its animation in app, and get an Island still/brief expressions when supported. No always-visible Island claim.

**Audience:** People who want a comforting custom companion close while studying, reading or focusing, including character and oshi fans who value personalization without complex setup.

**User-stated goal:** Animated Island character is the identity; occasional motion is an acceptable fallback if loops cannot be supported; companionship should **ideally be all-day or until the user stops**. **Approved design approach:** persistent Companion Mode ON/OFF in app + repeated, *user-initiated* genuine finite Live Activity windows of up to eight hours. Focus timer is optional and no longer the product identity. Pure decorative Live Activity eligibility and seamless renewal remain unverified/high-risk.

**First-session success to test:** In about one minute, a first-time user selects an original starter, enables Companion Mode and starts an Island task window on a compatible iPhone; they distinguish persistent Companion Mode selection from limited system visibility, and know how to end/renew Island display.

**Product principles:** Character-first; one-minute entry; truthful system behavior; accessible and low-energy motion; local privacy and ownership.

**MVP non-goals:** perpetual Island presence, unrestricted GIF rendering in system surfaces, account/backend/cloud, social features, commerce, AI art generation, complex animation tools and unlicensed franchise art. See [MVP scope](MVP_SCOPE.md).
